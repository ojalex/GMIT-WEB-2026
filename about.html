Background History of Got Matar.

Got Matar, meaning "Bare Hilltop" is an area of dispersed rural settlement in the Sub County of Bondo in Siaya County in the west of Kenya, near Lake Victoria, the source of the river Nile. Its economy is based on fishing in the Lake and on small –scale farming which is susceptible to periodic droughts.

The area is amongst the poorest in Kenya and suffered the highest level of HIV infection in all of Kenya around the turn of a century. AIDS killed many of the community’s working age population and left over one third of the children living as orphans. The economic problems facing the area had been exacerbated by a very high incidence of HIV infection,triggered in part by the cultural traditions of the dominant Luo population. The custom of “wife inheritance” offers avaluable form of insurance to bereaved widows in normal times but can be lethal when HIV appears on the scene.

In the above respect local community leaders under the leadership of Grace Ochieng' Andiki came up with the idea of forming Got Matar Community Development Group (GMCDG) in 2006 now a CBO to respond to the crisis. Following a series of consultation with potential donors, GMCDG began its operations with its main objective being, to ensure that school going age children in East Yimbo location access both Primary and Secondary Education plus skills training. See www.gotmatar.org for more information about Got Matar Community Development Group.

Got Matar Institute of Technology (IoT) was founded by the Got Matar Community Developement Group in 2012 as a community enterprise to enable young members of the community to acquire practical skills for which there was a growing demand in Kenya. It began with a dress-making and tailor course for about 30 students in the recently built Women's Centre.Since then it has grown rapidly and now registered, accredited and regulated by Technical and Vocational Education Training (TVET).

Mission Statement

Learning by Doing, Succeeding for Life.

To provide quality, competency-based technical and vocational education through innovative teaching, practical skills training, industry partnerships, and character development, empowering learners with the knowledge, skills, and values needed for employment, entrepreneurship, lifelong learning, and sustainable socio-economic development.**

Vision Statement

Inspiring Excellence, Shaping Tomorrow.

To be a leading Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) institution, recognized for excellence in skills development, innovation, and producing competent, ethical, and globally competitive graduates who transform society through knowledge, entrepreneurship, and sustainable development.**

Fig. 05 — Institute Timeline

Milestones

1998

GMIT founded

Opens with two departments — Mechanical and Civil — and a single academic block.

2004

Computer Science & Electronics added

Student strength crosses 1,000 with the addition of two new departments and a second hostel block.

2011

NAAC accreditation

GMIT receives its first institutional accreditation, formalising its academic review process.

2019

Central library rebuilt

A new three-storey library and digital resource centre opens on the north side of campus.

2026

Applied Robotics Centre opens

A dedicated research block for robotics and automation begins operating in Block C.
