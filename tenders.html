Notes for vendors

Submission

Sealed bid format

All bids must be submitted in a sealed envelope marked with the tender number, addressed to the Registrar's office.

Eligibility

Registered vendors only

Bidders must hold a valid registration and, where applicable, relevant trade licences for the work proposed.
