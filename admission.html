How to apply to GMIT

Admission to all UG programmes is through the state engineering entrance rank, followed by institute counselling. The process below runs in order — each step depends on the last.

01

Check Eligibility

Review minimum course requirements.

02

Register online

Fill out the digital form on the portal and pay the application fee via M-PESA or bank deposit. Or Download the admission form and submit it with the required documents. Click here to Download Admission Form

03

Upload Documents

Attach academic certificates, transcripts, national ID or birth certificate, and a passport photo.

04

Wait for Approval

The university reviews applications and issues admission letters to successful candidates.

05

Document verification & Fee Payment

Report to campus in person for final verification & fee paymentbefore classes begin.

Fig. 10 — Eligibility

Minimum requirements

10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, minimum 60% aggregate (55% for reserved categories), and a valid rank in the state engineering entrance examination.
