Fig. 02 — Programmes offered

Six departments. One standard of rigour.

Every programme runs a common first year of core mathematics and mechanics before students specialise — so a civil engineer and a coder start from the same drawing.

Dept. 01

Computer Science & Engineering

Systems, algorithms, and applied AI, taught alongside a mandatory hardware lab so software students understand what they're running on.

Duration4 Yrs · 8 Sem
Dept. 02

Electronics & Communication

Signal processing, embedded systems, and RF design, with a dedicated fabrication bay for student PCB runs.

Duration4 Yrs · 8 Sem
Dept. 03

Mechanical Engineering

Thermodynamics, CAD, and manufacturing, anchored by a working shop floor students use from second year onward.

Duration4 Yrs · 8 Sem
Dept. 04

Civil Engineering

Structures, geotechnics, and surveying, with field practicals run on the institute's own construction sites.

Duration4 Yrs · 8 Sem
Dept. 05

Electrical Engineering

Power systems, control theory, and machines, taught with access to the campus's own substation for live study.

Duration4 Yrs · 8 Sem
Dept. 06

Information Technology

Networks, databases, and software engineering practice, run as studio-style project courses from year two.

Duration4 Yrs · 8 Sem
