Fig. 22 — Take the Tour

Around the campus

Main Gate & Grounds

The main entrance, with visitor parking and the approach to the Admin Block.

Academic Block

Lecture halls and faculty offices across three floors, built around a central courtyard.

Classrooms

Smart-board equipped rooms seating 40–60 students, used across all diploma programmes.

Computer Lab

Networked workstations for coursework, practicals and project work.

Workshop & Labs

Mechanical, electrical and civil labs with recently commissioned equipment.

Library

Reading hall, reference section, and a quiet study wing open through office hours.

Hostel

On-campus housing for out-of-town students, with separate blocks and a common mess hall.

Sports Ground

Open field for athletics and team sports, used for inter-department tournaments.

Auditorium

Seats 400 for convocation, guest lectures and cultural events.

Convocation Day

Graduating students and faculty at the annual convocation held in the auditorium.

Sports Day

Inter-department athletics and team events held on the campus sports ground.

Cultural Fest

Student-run performances, exhibitions and stalls held each spring.

Orientation Day

Incoming students and families meeting faculty and touring the campus.
